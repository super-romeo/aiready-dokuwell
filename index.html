How it works

An independent AI consultant spends a large part of the week on discovery: understanding how a prospect works, what data they have, which tasks eat their time and whether they are ready for an AI project at all. Much of that first conversation can be done before the call. ReadyScore gives you a branded assessment that collects these answers and turns them into a report the prospect wants to read.

You start by setting up your brand: logo, colors, your domain and the name the prospect will see. The assessment comes with a ready set of questions about the business, its processes, data, current tools and team, and you can add your own questions for the industries you work with. Each assessment is a link you send by email, post after a webinar or put on your website.

The prospect answers in about fifteen minutes, without creating an account. When they finish, ReadyScore prepares a PDF report under your brand: readiness scores by area, the gaps that hold the business back, a few quick wins and a suggested first project. The prospect receives the report, and you receive it too, with their answers.

The report is written to start a conversation, not to replace your work. It shows the prospect that you understand their situation and gives you a concrete agenda for the first paid meeting. You can edit the recommendations before the report goes out, or let it go out automatically.

Your prospect list shows who opened the link, who finished, and the scores of each. Prospects with clear gaps and high readiness are at the top, so you know whom to call first. You can export the list to your CRM.

Questions and recommendations can be adapted by industry: a dental group, a law firm and a logistics company need different questions. You keep several assessments and send the one that fits.

Setup takes an afternoon. You add your brand, review the standard questions, write a few sentences about how you work, and send the first link the same day. Every report you send carries your name, so prospects remember who helped them think it through.

ReadyScore is built for solo consultants and small consultancies that sell AI projects to small and mid-sized businesses. It is a tool for discovery and reports, not a project management system.

ReadyScore does not give legal, security or compliance advice. The recommendations in each report are a starting point for your own professional judgement.
